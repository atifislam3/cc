Quick Reference
What to Look For
FVG
Fair Value Gap

Look for 3-candle patterns with gaps between C1 and C3 wicks

BOS
Break of Structure

Watch for breaks of swing highs/lows with body close

CHoCH
Change of Character

First break against the current trend direction

OB
Order Blocks

Last opposite candle before impulsive move

LIQ
Liquidity

Equal highs/lows, swing points with stop clusters
