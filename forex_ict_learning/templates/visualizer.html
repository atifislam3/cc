How It Works

The visualizer automatically scans the chart data and highlights ICT patterns:

  • FVG: 3-candle gaps where wicks don't overlap
  • BOS: Breaks of recent swing highs/lows
  • OB: Last opposite candle before impulse
  • LIQ: Equal highs/lows (stop clusters)
